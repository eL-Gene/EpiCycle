================================================================================
NOTICE -- code adapted from scikit-image in walls.py
================================================================================

The ridge wall pictures in `walls.py` (`ridge_filter` and its helpers:
`_ridge_hessian`, `_ridge_eigvals`, `_frangi_scales`, `_meijering_scales`,
`_ridge_float_type`) reproduce, operation for operation, scikit-image's
`skimage.filters.frangi`, `skimage.filters.meijering`, the Hessian and
eigenvalue helpers of `skimage/feature/corner.py` and the float-type rule of
`skimage/_shared/utils.py`, rescheduled (a Stop between scales, the passes on
threads) but with the same arithmetic.
