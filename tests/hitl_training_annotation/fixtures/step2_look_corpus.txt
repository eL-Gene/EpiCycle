## 1. The states

One page header, workspace bar, hint and routing line sit above the accordion in every state (unchanged; drawn text "2D Model HITL Bootstrapping" / "Workspace: {path} (shared across steps)" / "Select a patch → curate slices in the active view → finalize → train a round" / "Routing: XY → lateral, XZ/YZ → pooled axial."). The accordion keeps the shared Accordion; the default open section stays Curation. Every state must fit the 578 px dock with no horizontal scrollbar (Step 3's I6 invariant, measured in a subprocess, §6) and use the room at 1000 px (napari caps the dock at 976).

### 1.0 The room (D26)

Measured, not assumed. The dock is ~576 px tall (the bottom "Run + Artifact History" dock eats the rest) and ~578 px wide (976 at 1000 asked, napari's cap). The page header, workspace bar, hint, routing line and the accordion's headers leave ~290 px for the open section (the Curation body starts ~208 px below the dock top in the 578 shots, with two headers under it). The accordion makes each section a scroll area sized to its largest minimum; the gotcha "A whole-section scroll area hides the lock line at the dock width — pin the column feet, scroll the columns" (Step 3 Model Refinement look) is the lesson.
The Model Refinement solution, cited. A horizontal QSplitter (left min 240, right min 300, handle 6, stretch 0:1; fusion_reconstruction/ui.py:93-97); each column is a scroll area over a pinned foot; the foot sits on a vertical _CompactSplitter (ui.py:347-355, which asks for no more height than its panes' minimums; setChildrenCollapsible(False), stretch 1 / 0; splitter at :3245-3262) with the scroll's floor MR_SPLIT_TOP_MIN_H = 120 (:189, :3243), the foot's floor its minimumSizeHint, and a Loss plot floor MR_LOSS_MIN_H = 140 (:187); the lock line is the FIRST row of the section, outside the scroll areas, so it never scrolls away. Step 2 copies the shape.
