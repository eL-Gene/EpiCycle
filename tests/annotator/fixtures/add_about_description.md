### 4.10
 The description, verbatim (parity test `test_add_help_parity.py` reads it out of this section, as `test_refine_help_parity.py:250` does)

> **Fills empty space inside a box with new objects — many at once. Existing objects never lose a voxel, and nothing is written until you press Accept.**

> <b>Nuclei</b> finds bright objects by brightness alone and suits a nuclear stain. <b>Cells with a membrane stain</b> grows one object from each marker until it meets a wall in the membrane channel or dark space; markers come from another run's objects, from nuclei in a second channel, or from your clicks, and it measures in microns, from Step 3's Spacing. New objects are written only where there is no object yet, so clear the masks you do not want first. Objects touching the box edge and very small objects start unticked.

`HELP_LINES` / `ITEM_HELP` get an entry for **every** Add control: `add_preset_combo`, `add_start_btn`, S / M / L, `add_numbers_btn`, `add_recenter_btn`, `add_marker_source_combo`, `add_nuclei_channel_combo`, `add_nuclei_threshold_sb`, `add_nuclei_auto_btn`, `add_place_markers_btn`, `add_click_undo_btn`, `add_click_clear_btn`, `add_click_list` (§12.5), `add_wall_channel_combo`, the four prepare ticks, `add_wall_kind_combo`, `add_channel_combo`, `add_clahe_cb` (Local contrast), `add_threshold_sb`, `add_otsu_btn` (Auto), `add_preview_btn`, `add_stop_btn`, `add_all_btn` / `add_none_btn`, `add_show_walls_cb`, `add_contour_cb` (§12.3), every Advanced row of §4.1 (click ball radius, shrink run objects, dark space reach, dark below, separate touching objects, spacing, local contrast strength, untick at the box edge + edge zone, smallest object, smooth + smoothing), `add_batch_refine_btn`, `add_refine_piece_btn`, `add_again_btn`, `add_undo_all_btn`, `add_accept_refine_btn` (§12.4). Tooltip-only exceptions, named: the preset combo's item tooltips, and the six sliders inside Numbers…. **Each µm/voxel sentence is checked numerically** on a synthetic object in `test_add_help_parity.py`: a 2.0 µm radius ball at 0.517 µm spacing has every voxel centre within 2.0 µm of the click and spans at least `2 × (2.0 − 0.517)` µm along each axis ("about 4 µm across"); a 1.0 µm erosion removes the expected shell; the dark clearance reaches 2 µm; the Cells limit refuses 4,000,001; a nuclei piece whose deepest voxel is 0.9 µm from a kept voxel is skipped and one at 1.1 µm kept.
